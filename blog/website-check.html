Website check: Google, AI and customers

Enter your website address and see three things in 20 seconds: whether Google finds you, whether AI assistants can see and recommend you, and whether the site brings in customers. A score from 0 to 100, what is missing, and the full report by email. Free.

23 November 2026•1 min to run•Aris Alampourinos

Until recently it was enough for Google to find you. Today more and more people ask ChatGPT, Gemini or Perplexity “who should I hire for…” and get a ready answer with two or three names. If those tools cannot read your site, you are not among those names, however good your work is.

This check reads your site the way Google does, the way an AI assistant does and the way a customer does, and tells you what is missing for each. It works for any business: offices, shops, freelancers, holiday rentals. If the site is a restaurant, café or bar, it also checks the menu, opening hours and bookings.

What it checks

  • Can Google find you? Title and description in results, main heading, amount of text, sitemap, whether Google may show the site, image descriptions and structured data.
  • Do AI assistants recommend you? Whether your site lets ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and Gemini read it, whether the text is visible without JavaScript, whether the business is described in a form machines understand, and whether there are questions and answers, an About page, links to your profiles and an llms.txt file.
  • Does it bring in customers? Contact details, a button for the next step, tap-to-call, a form, reviews, prices and how convincing the text is.
  • Do visitors trust it? Secure connection, whether it looks up to date, privacy policy, business email, company details.
  • On a phone. Whether the page fits the screen and how quickly it opens.

Why AI visibility matters

AI assistants do not “search” the way Google does. They read pages and write an answer. To put you in that answer they need to be able to read your site, understand which business you are and what exactly you do, and find written answers to the questions people ask. Many sites, without their owners knowing, forbid AI tools from reading them, or load their text in a way those tools cannot see.

How the score works

Each point counts according to how much it affects the result. The overall score is the average of the three: Google, AI and customers. Anything only partly there gets half the points. Above 80 means the basics are right. Below 60, your site is losing work every day.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to give you passwords or access to my site?
No. The check only reads what is public: the home page, up to five more pages, robots.txt and llms.txt.

How do you know whether AI tools can read my site?
We check your site’s settings for the bots of ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude and Gemini, whether the text exists without JavaScript, and whether the business is described in a way machines understand. We cannot see what an AI answers to every question, but we can see whether you give it the chance to recommend you.

Do you keep the address or the results?
No. Only if you ask for the report by email do we receive your email address, the business name if you give one, and the results, so we can send it. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Does it work for sites on Wix, WordPress, Shopify or other platforms?
Yes, for any public website. Some sites block automated checks. If that happens, send me a message and I will check it myself.

Is this the same as Google PageSpeed?
No. PageSpeed mostly measures speed. This check looks at whether Google and AI assistants find you and whether visitors turn into customers.

Can you fix it for us?
Yes. We build new websites and fix existing ones so they show up on Google, are read by AI assistants and bring in customers. The first 30-minute call is free.

This is an indicative check based on the public pages of the site at the time of the check. It does not measure Google ranking, traffic or what AI tools answer to specific questions.
