    How the result works

    Each answer carries a weight, depending on how often that situation leads to fines in practice. A venue that opens next month, run by an owner who does not read Greek, with nobody looking after the paperwork, is far more exposed than a venue open for years with an accountant and a food technologist, even if both are run with the same care. The result is low, medium or high risk, with the factors behind it.

    What is at stake

    Penalties in Greek food businesses are set by different authorities. A few examples, as of November 2026: €10,500 for each undeclared worker, an immediate 48-hour closure when receipts are not issued, and fines of up to €20,000 for food hygiene breaches or for operating outside what was notified. The full report lists them with their sources.

    If you are opening a new venue, the order of licences, staff and inspections is in our guide to opening a restaurant in Greece. For food safety records, see HACCP for restaurants in Greece.

    Frequently asked questions

    Who inspects restaurants in Greece?
    Food hygiene is checked by EFET, the Hellenic Food Authority, and by the public health services of the regions. Staff are checked by the Labour Inspectorate, receipts by AADE, the tax authority, and the municipality checks the venue's notification. They work separately, so one visit can be followed by another.

    Why does the check ask about my situation and not about specific documents?
    Because in practice fines follow situations: little time before opening, documents in a language you do not read, paperwork nobody has taken on. Those are what decide whether gaps get caught in time.

    Is anything I enter saved or sent to you?
    Your answers stay in your browser while you use the check. Only if you ask for the full report by email do we receive your email address, the venue name if you give one, and your answers, so we can send it. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

    Does a low risk result mean I will pass an inspection?
    No. It means your situation is well covered. Only an inspection, or a mock inspection, shows how a specific venue stands.
