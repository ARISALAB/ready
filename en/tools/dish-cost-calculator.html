How the price is calculated

For each ingredient the tool first finds the real cost: the purchase price divided by the yield. If sea bream costs €9.00/kg and 55% is left after cleaning, the real cost is €16.36/kg. That is then multiplied by the quantity in the recipe.

The total of all ingredients, divided by the number of portions, plus any extras, gives the cost per portion. The suggested price comes from your target food cost:

Price excl. VAT = Cost per portion ÷ Target food cost
Price incl. VAT = Price excl. VAT × (1 + VAT)

The suggested price is rounded up to the next 10 cents. It is a starting point: the final price also depends on your market, how popular the dish is and its role on the menu. If you are setting up a new venue, see our guide to opening a restaurant in Greece.

What the colours mean

Good: food cost is at or below 80% of your target. Caution: it is close to target, so a supplier price rise would push it over. Over target: the dish costs more than its price can carry.

Frequently asked questions

Do I enter purchase prices with or without VAT?
Without VAT. Input VAT on purchases is reclaimed, so including it inflates your cost.

Is my data stored anywhere?
No. Everything is calculated in your browser and nothing is sent to us. To keep a costing, download the PDF.

What target food cost should I use?
For most restaurants the average is 28–32%. Drinks and coffee run much lower, seafood usually higher.

Want us to look at it together?

We cost your whole menu on site and give you, in writing, the prices that leave a profit.
