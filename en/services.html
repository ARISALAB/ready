Services

Services for restaurants, tourism and hospitality

Every engagement starts with a site visit. Choose one service or we combine them into a complete plan.

01

Opening & re-opening a venue

We plan and supervise the whole preparation of a new venue, or the re-opening of one after a closure, so it opens organised from day one.

  • Supervision of renovation and preparation
  • Staffing and organisation chart
  • Re-opening after a closure

02

Menu & pricing

We create or rework your menu based on the real cost of every dish, your audience and market conditions.

  • Menu creation and redesign
  • Dish and drink costing
  • Pricing policy
  • Food and wine pairing

03

Staff training

We train waiters, hosts, cashiers and maîtres d'hôtel, using a certified adult-training methodology and hands-on practice in your venue.

  • Floor service and upselling
  • Guest reception and bookings
  • Cash desk procedures
  • Onboarding new staff

04

Operations

We set clear roles, the right division of work and a clean order flow between kitchen and floor, for faster and more consistent service.

  • Kitchen and floor order flow
  • Division of work and shifts
  • Guest service standards

Financial management & cash control

  • Cash and receipts control

  • Financial management of the venue

Stock & suppliers
